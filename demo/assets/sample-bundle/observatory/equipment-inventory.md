# Equipment Inventory

## Purpose
The equipment inventory is the current map of observatory hardware, consumables, and loaned accessories. It lets operators find a compatible component quickly and gives maintenance staff a traceable history when something fails.

## Required fields
Give every item a stable inventory identifier and record manufacturer, model, serial number, location, owner, condition, and last inspection date. For cameras and mounts, include firmware version and the cables or adapters required for normal operation. Mark safety-critical items such as ladders, extinguishers, and lifting straps with inspection due dates. A missing serial number is recorded as unknown rather than guessed from a label or purchase order.

## Updates and checks
Update the record when an item moves, is repaired, loaned, retired, or found damaged. Attach a short note and work-order reference to each change. At the start of each quarter, compare the list with a physical count and resolve duplicate identifiers. Quarantine unsafe equipment with a visible tag and remove it from the available pool. Before an observing run, the operator checks the requested setup against this inventory and reports substitutions in the night log.
