# Dome Shutdown

## Purpose
Dome shutdown leaves the observatory dry, powered safely, and ready for the next crew. The duty astronomer starts it after the final exposure, or immediately when a weather gate requires closure.

## Sequence
Stop acquisitions and wait for the camera to finish writing. Park the mount at its marked home position, return the instrument selector to its safe setting, and cap the telescope and finder. Remove temporary cables from walkways and secure the remaining loops so the dome cannot catch them. Close and latch the shutter, then switch off nonessential instrument power. Keep ventilation or environmental controls running when required by the camera or building plan; shutdown is not the same as turning off every breaker.

## Verification
Walk the room with a flashlight: check that no person or loose object remains in the rotation path, the floor is dry, emergency access is clear, and the rain sensor is unobstructed. Confirm the shutter indicator agrees with the physical latch and record the final humidity, temperature, mount state, and time. If any step fails, leave the affected system in its safest known state, call the site lead, and document the fault before leaving.
