# Weather Gates

## Purpose
Weather gates define when the dome may open, remain open, or close. They protect people and equipment while giving the duty astronomer predictable decisions instead of relying on intuition during a changing night.

## Opening gate
Open only when the forecast and local sensors agree on a dry, stable window. Require no precipitation, relative humidity below 80 percent, wind below 25 km/h, and a temperature-dew-point spread above 3 °C. Check the all-sky camera for cloud movement and inspect the rain sensor for debris. A clear forecast never overrides a wet sensor or a rapidly approaching storm cell.

## Monitoring and closure
Sample sensors every minute and display both current values and ten-minute trends. Enter a warning state at 75 percent humidity, 20 km/h wind, or a 4 °C dew-point spread; pause new exposures and prepare to close. Close the dome immediately for detected rain, lightning within 15 km, wind above 35 km/h, or a failing sensor that prevents safe assessment. Record the trigger, timestamp, and recovery decision. Reopening requires ten dry minutes and a fresh walk-around inspection.
