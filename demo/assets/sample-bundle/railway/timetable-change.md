# Timetable Change

## Validate the change
Control compares the amended timetable with the current service plan, traincrew diagrams, rollingstock availability, engineering possessions, and station staffing. Confirm the effective date, service identifiers, public calling pattern, pathing constraints, and any temporary speed restrictions. A change is not operationally usable until a named owner confirms the source and version.

## Brief the railway
Publish one controlled notice rather than sending conflicting extracts. Brief signallers, drivers, guards, dispatchers, station teams, maintenance leads, and passenger-information staff using the same service identity and times. Ask each role to repeat the part that affects its work: crossing arrangements, platform, turnback, connections, or last-train commitment. Mark superseded copies and remove them from shared boards and cab packs.

## Monitor first use
For the first affected operating period, control watches departures, platform conflicts, crew readiness, and passenger messages. Log deviations against the amended plan, not against an obsolete schedule. At the review point, retain the approved notice, record unresolved impacts, and either confirm the change or issue a corrected version with a new effective time.
