# Depot Closeout

## Secure the site
The duty lead checks that every vehicle is on its assigned road, stationary protection is applied, and movements are complete. Confirm that pits, walkways, fueling points, lifting equipment, and isolation boundaries are clear. Remove tools and temporary barriers only when the responsible maintainer releases them. Close doors and gates, inspect lighting and alarms, and report spills, leaks, or damaged fencing before leaving the area.

## Reconcile the work
Compare the vehicle position board with the actual consist and record mileage, fuel or charge state, defects, isolation tags, and deferred work. Each open defect needs an owner, safe operating limit, and next action; “watch” is not a substitute for a decision. Attach test sheets and parts information to the correct vehicle record. Escalate anything that could affect the first diagram to control and the incoming maintenance lead.

## Hand over
Brief the next shift on vehicles unavailable, equipment isolated, unusual noises, contractors on site, and inspections due. Read back critical restrictions, sign the closeout log, and keep the radio available until the handoff is accepted.
