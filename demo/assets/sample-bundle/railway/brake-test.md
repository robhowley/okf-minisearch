# Brake Test

## Set up
Confirm the consist list, brake system, cab controls, and required test type with the driver or train preparer. Apply handbrakes or other stationary protection before connecting or separating vehicles. Walk the train with a radio and identify the last vehicle. Check that hoses are coupled, cocks are correctly positioned, reservoirs are charged, and visible brake indicators agree with the intended state. Keep clear of couplers and moving equipment.

## Test and verify
The driver applies and releases the train brake using the prescribed sequence. The tester observes the response at the leading, middle, and last vehicle or at the locations specified for that stock. Report a delayed application, incomplete release, audible leak, isolated vehicle, or conflicting indicator immediately. Repeat a test only after the cause and protection are understood; a repeated pass does not erase an unexplained failure.

## Release the train
Record test type, time, consist, tester, result, and any restriction. The driver signs acceptance only when the full train is protected by the tested brake system. If the result is incomplete, hold the departure and call control for technical advice.
