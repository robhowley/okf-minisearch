# Signal Inspection

## Before approaching
Review the work notice, signal identification, control area, and protection granted by the signaller. Carry a radio, identification light, and the latest defect sheet. Do not enter the track or signal structure until the signaller repeats the protection and the inspector reads it back. Treat an unexpected aspect, dark lamp, or conflicting instruction as a failure rather than a nuisance.

## Field checks
From the normal viewing distance, verify the signal head, route indicator, sighting line, and surrounding obstruction. Compare the displayed aspect with the signaller’s stated route; do not test by requesting a movement unless the work notice authorizes it. Check lenses for damage, condensation, dirt, or a loose hood. At the equipment case, look for water ingress, heat marks, exposed wiring, and an unlocked door. Record signal ID, aspect, time, weather, and exact symptom. Photograph only when permitted by the work instruction.

## Closeout
Report defects immediately over the designated channel, then repeat the restriction or protection that follows. Leave covers secured and tools counted. The signaller closes the inspection only after receiving the written finding and confirming whether the signal is serviceable, restricted, or out of use.
