# Platform Dispatch

## Prepare the departure
The dispatcher checks the working timetable, platform indicator, booked calling points, and any active operating notice. Confirm that the driver has the correct service identity and departure time. Scan the platform from the rear of the train toward the stopping point: passengers must be clear of doors, ramps must be withdrawn, and no object may project into the train path. Resolve a crowd, unattended item, or door fault before giving a signal.

## Give and monitor the signal
Make the departure indication using the agreed hand signal, lamp, or dispatch equipment while maintaining a clear view of the train. The driver acknowledges using the prescribed method. Watch the first vehicle roll away and check that every door is closed, the platform edge is clear, and no person is running alongside. If visibility is lost, the driver reports an abnormal condition, or a door reopens, instruct the driver to stop and inform the signaller.

## Record exceptions
Log late dispatch, assistance provided, door defects, and any stop instruction. Pass a concise update to control so the next station knows what to expect. Never trade a safety check for timetable recovery.
