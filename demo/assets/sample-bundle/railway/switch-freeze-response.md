# Switch Freeze Response

## Protect the movement
When points fail to prove or move in freezing conditions, stop movements over the affected route. Tell the signaller the point number, last known position, attempted command, and whether a vehicle is fouling the clearance. The signaller protects the route and confirms the restriction. Do not force a switch with a locomotive, kick snow into the mechanism, or rely on an unverified indication.

## Clear and assess
A qualified points technician or authorized response crew inspects the switch under the granted protection. Remove loose snow and ice with approved tools, keeping hands clear of the stretcher and drive rods. Check the stock rail, stretcher, detection contacts, and drainage for compacted material or standing water. If heat tracing is fitted, confirm its status through the approved control rather than opening electrical equipment in wet conditions. Attempt a movement only after the technician and signaller agree that the route is protected.

## Restore service
Run the prescribed proving cycle and record the final position. If detection remains unreliable, impose the published temporary speed or hand-signalling arrangement. Notify control, affected crews, and passenger information staff with the next review time.
