# Greenhouse Basics

A small greenhouse extends the growing season, but sunlight can make it dangerously hot. Monitor it rather than using it as storage. Keep a thermometer at plant height, assign opening and closing duties, and post a checklist by the door.

## Control Temperature and Air

Open vents or doors early when the inside temperature begins rising, and close them before a cold night. Use shade cloth during intense summer sun, leaving enough light for sturdy growth. Air movement reduces fungal disease; a fan should circulate air without drying seedlings. Do not crowd trays against glazing or block vents with supplies.

## Water and Organize

Water in the morning so foliage and benches dry before evening. Check each tray individually because different crops and container sizes lose moisture at different rates. Keep hoses off walkways, store tools clean, and raise seedling trays from the floor to improve drainage. Label every flat with crop, variety, sowing date, and owner or destination bed.

## Clean Between Uses

Remove dead leaves and spilled mix immediately. At the end of a crop cycle, empty benches, wash trays, and disinfect reusable containers with an approved solution. Inspect glazing, screens, and heaters before the season starts. If a plant shows an unfamiliar disease or insect outbreak, isolate the tray and notify the greenhouse steward before moving anything else.
