# Wheel Maintenance

## Daily Cleanup

Switch off and unplug the wheel before removing splash pans or reaching near the belt. Scrape wet clay into the reclaim bin while it is still soft; never wash chunks into the drain. Wipe the pan, bat pins, wheel head, seat, and nearby floor with a damp sponge. Check that the foot pedal is dry and that the power cord is not under a chair or table leg.

## Weekly Service

The studio steward should remove the splash pan, clean the drain channel, and inspect the belt area for packed clay. Tighten loose bat pins and chair hardware with the correct tool. Listen for grinding, pulsing speed, unusual heat, or wobble during a short empty test. Keep lubricants and water away from motor openings unless the manufacturer's instructions specifically allow them.

## Faults and Handover

Tag a wheel that behaves unpredictably and move its plug to the off position. Write the wheel number, symptom, date, and last user in the maintenance log. Do not open electrical covers or return the wheel to service until a qualified person has checked it.
