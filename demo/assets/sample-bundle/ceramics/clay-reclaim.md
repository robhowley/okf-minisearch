# Clay Reclaim

## Sort Before Slaking

Collect trimmings, failed pieces, and throwing scraps in the labeled reclaim buckets. Remove plaster, bisque, wood, glaze chips, and plastic before adding anything. Keep white, buff, red, and dark bodies separate unless the studio manager has approved a mixed batch. Clay that has touched glaze should go into the waste stream because fired colorants can contaminate new work.

## Reclaim Workflow

Break dry scraps into pieces smaller than a fist and cover them with water. Let the bucket stand until the pieces are soft throughout, then pour off excess water and blend the slurry with the reclaim mixer. Spread the mixture on plaster bats or a canvas board in a thin layer. Turn it when the surface firms, wedge it when it reaches a workable consistency, and return it to an airtight bag.

## Drying and Return

Mark each bag with body type and date. Test a small lump before restocking; it should wedge smoothly without sour odor, hard cores, or gritty inclusions. Quarantine questionable batches for review rather than mixing them into clean stock.
