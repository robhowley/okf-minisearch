# Glaze Mixing

## Gather and Check

Read the recipe card and verify that every ingredient is present, correctly labeled, and within its approved storage area. Wear the studio respirator or mask, gloves, eye protection, and apron required for powdered materials. Record the batch size, lot numbers, mixer setting, and date before opening bags. Never substitute an ingredient without a revised test plan.

## Mix and Sieve

Measure powders on the designated scale, keeping the container on its tray to catch spills. Add water to the mixing bucket before powders, then blend slowly until no dry pockets remain. Pass the slurry through the specified sieve, working in small portions and rinsing the screen between colors. Measure specific gravity or viscosity when the recipe calls for it, and adjust only with documented additions.

## Label and Store

Put the glaze name, batch number, firing range, hazards, and mixing date on the bucket. Keep a retained test tile and a small sample when the batch is new. Seal the lid, wipe the rim, and return tools to the glaze area. Log settling, crawling, unusual color, or contamination before anyone applies the batch.
