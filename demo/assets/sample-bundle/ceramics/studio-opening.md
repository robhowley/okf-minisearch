# Studio Opening

## Enter and Inspect

Unlock the studio using the current access procedure and sign in. Walk the room before turning on equipment. Look for water on the floor, blocked exits, damaged cords, kiln alarms, chemical spills, and tools left in unsafe places. Photograph or isolate anything that could injure someone, then contact the designated lead instead of quietly working around it.

## Start Shared Equipment

Check that ventilation is clear and operating before opening glaze materials. Turn on only the lights, wheels, slab roller, or other equipment needed for the session. Confirm that the kiln area is restricted while a firing is hot, and review the firing sheet for an active program. Keep doors and fire extinguishers accessible throughout the day.

## Prepare Work Areas

Restock clean bats, ribs, sponges, and hand-washing supplies. Set out damp-cleaning tools rather than dry brooms. Confirm that reclaim, scrap, glaze, and general waste containers are labeled and not full. Note shortages on the studio board, then welcome arriving members with a reminder about orientation requirements and today's hazards.
