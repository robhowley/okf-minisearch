# Kiln Loading

## Prepare Shelves

Confirm the firing type, target cone, and kiln shelf size before moving ware. Brush loose dust from shelves outdoors or with the studio's approved damp method. Check that each shelf is flat, dry, and free of glaze drips. Add posts of equal height at three or four stable points, leaving enough clearance for heat circulation and the lid.

## Arrange Ware

Load only fully dry pieces with no trapped water, damp clay, or unfired glaze on their bases. Keep glazed surfaces from touching shelves, posts, or neighboring work. Place taller forms toward the center when possible, and leave a finger-width gap around pieces so they can expand and contract. Use kiln cookies under pieces that may run, but do not hide a damaged foot beneath one.

## Close and Record

Photograph the finished load if the arrangement is unusual. Record shelf levels, owner names, firing program, and any known risks on the kiln sheet. Inspect the lid seal and element path, then ask the firing lead to approve the load before starting it.
